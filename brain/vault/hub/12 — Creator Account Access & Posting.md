# 12 — Creator Account Access & Posting

Notion page under [[NOOCAP Systems — SOP Library]], last edited 2026-11-08.

Loom video: [ADD Loom link]
In one line: We manage all our creators' social accounts from one place using manager access, so we never need anyone's personal password.

### What this system does
We post and manage content for many creators, and asking for their passwords is risky for everyone. Instead, creators give NOOCAP manager access. Their Facebook and Instagram accounts sit inside our Meta Business Suite portfolio, and their YouTube channels are managed through our collab account, which is connected to Metricool for posting.

### Who is involved
- Owner: The Ops Manager
- Users: anyone who posts or schedules content for creators

### Adding a new creator
- Ask the creator to add NOOCAP's Meta business portfolio as a partner on their Facebook page and Instagram [ADD how-to link].
- Ask them to add noocapcollab@gmail.com as a manager on their YouTube channel.
- Connect their accounts in Metricool so we can schedule posts.
- Add them to the table above.

### Rules
- Never ask a creator for their Gmail or social media password.
- Creators always keep ownership of their own channels, and we only ever need manager access.
- Only post what has been approved (Green Light in SOP 02).
