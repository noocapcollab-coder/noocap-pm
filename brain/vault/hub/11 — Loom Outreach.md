# 11 — Loom Outreach

Notion page under [[NOOCAP Systems — SOP Library]], last edited 2026-11-08.

Loom video: [ADD Loom link]
In one line: We record one master video, and the system makes a personalised version for every prospect with their own Instagram, YouTube or website showing in the background.

### What this system does
A personal video gets far more replies than a plain message, but recording one for every prospect takes forever. So we record one square master video once, and the system creates a version for each prospect with their own social profile or website behind it, plus a short pre-recorded clip. It looks like we made the video just for them.

### Who is involved
- Owner: The Ops Manager
- Users: whoever handles outreach

### When it runs
Whenever we have a new batch of prospects to reach out to.

### Tools used

### Step by step
- Add each prospect to the Google Sheet [ADD sheet link] with their name and links (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, website).
- Run the video automation, which creates one video per prospect.
- Watch a few of the finished videos to check the backgrounds loaded correctly.
- Upload each video to Loom by hand.
- Send each prospect their own Loom link with a short message.

### Rules
- Every link in the sheet must open properly, or that prospect's video will have a broken background.
- Spot-check videos before uploading, because one wrong name or background ruins the personal feel.
- Log who you've sent videos to, so nobody gets messaged twice.

### 🎙️ Loom script (about 2 minutes)
  [SHOW: a finished personalised video]
  This video explains our Loom outreach system. What you're watching looks like a video made just for one prospect, with their own Instagram showing in the background, but it was actually recorded only once.
  [SHOW: the Google Sheet]
  It starts with this Google Sheet, where every prospect has a row with their name and links like their Instagram, YouTube, TikTok or website. We recorded one square master video and a short pre-recorded clip, and those stay the same for everyone.
  [SHOW: running the automation and the output folder]
  An automation we built with Claude Code goes through the sheet row by row and creates a separate video for each prospect, putting their own profile or site behind the master video.
  [SHOW: uploading to Loom]
  The finished videos get spot-checked and then uploaded to Loom by hand, and each prospect gets their own link with a short message. The thing that breaks this most often is a dead link in the sheet, since that leaves a blank background in that person's video.
