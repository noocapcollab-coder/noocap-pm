# 07 — Carousel Automation

Notion page under [[NOOCAP Systems — SOP Library]], last edited 2026-11-08.

Loom video: [ADD Loom link]
In one line: We find carousels that are already doing well in the AI space, rewrite them in each client's voice and style, and Metricool posts them Monday to Friday on Instagram and LinkedIn.

### What this system does
Carousels are the swipeable image posts on Instagram and LinkedIn. Instead of guessing what will work, we use the Top Carousels tool to find AI carousels that are already performing well, then we rewrite the same strong hook in our client's own voice and design style. Every client has their own look, and the carousels are always about Claude and making money with AI. Metricool then posts them on a schedule.

### Who is involved
- Owner: The Ops Manager
- Users: the content team
- First client on it: Lindsay (paper-style design with the orange fox mascot)

### When it runs
One carousel per client, posted Monday to Friday.

### Tools used

### Step by step
- Find a trending carousel for the client's topic using Top Carousels.
- Rewrite the hook and slides in the client's voice, keeping the idea but making it fully our own.
- Generate the slides in the client's design style.
- Check every slide for spelling, facts and readability on a phone screen.
- Schedule it in Metricool for the next open weekday, with Instagram and Facebook posted together as one post and LinkedIn alongside.
- If the carousel has a comment keyword, set up its ManyChat flow (SOP 06).

### Rules
- Never copy a carousel word for word, because we only borrow the idea and the hook structure.
- Stick to each client's own design style, since every client has a different look.
- Topics stay on Claude and making money with AI.
