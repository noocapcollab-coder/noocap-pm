# 10 — Cold Email Outreach

Notion page under [[NOOCAP Systems — SOP Library]], last edited 2026-11-08.

Loom video: [ADD Loom link]
In one line: We send cold emails to potential clients through Instantly from Pratham's NOOCAP inboxes, and anyone who replies (on email or LinkedIn) gets followed up by the team.

### What this system does
This is how we find new clients for NOOCAP. We load a list of potential clients into Instantly, which sends personalised emails from several NOOCAP inboxes on our noocap.co.in domain under Pratham's name. Using several inboxes keeps each one sending a small, safe amount so emails land in the inbox and not in spam.

### Who is involved
- Owner: The Ops Manager (setup) and Pratham (the sender)
- Users: whoever handles replies

### When it runs
Campaigns run every weekday once they're switched on.

### Step by step
- Build or get the prospect list [ADD where lists live].
- Upload the list into a campaign in Instantly.
- Check the email copy and the follow-up steps.
- Make sure the campaign is sending from Pratham's inboxes, then switch it on.
- Check Instantly every day for replies and answer them quickly.
- Check LinkedIn too, since some prospects reply there instead.

### Rules
- Never send from Pratham's main inbox, only from the outreach inboxes.
- Don't raise the daily sending limit without asking the Ops Manager, because it can hurt delivery.
- Reply to interested prospects the same day.
