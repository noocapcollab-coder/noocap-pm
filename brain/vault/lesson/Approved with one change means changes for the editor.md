When: A brand approves a cut but asks for one change in the same email (music, a caption, a frame)
Do: Put the video's EDIT TRACKER row on Changes with the brand's words in Brand Feedback and the board card on 8- Changes, so the editor fixes it and the revised cut goes straight back to the brand. Anything else in the email (billing details, a date) is handled on its own.
Never: Hold the whole email for Harsh because the readers disagree on approval vs feedback, or treat it as a full approval and post the cut unchanged.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
