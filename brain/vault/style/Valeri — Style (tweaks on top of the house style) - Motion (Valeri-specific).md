- **Zoom: slow and premium.** Ease-in-out over 1.2–2 s, scale 1 → 1.15 max, onto the exact thing (the GitHub repo name, the key line). **No fast or generic punch-zooms.**
- **Cursor clicks:** realistic macOS cursor moves to the field, clicks (press + ripple), and the prompt types in (i01).
- **Emphasis pop-ups** for key phrases ("completely free", "$0"): a card or stamp springs in over the scene, holds, then clears.
- **Seamless transitions:** match-cut and zoom-through between scenes; white ↔ black via a logo zoom (i02). Prefer these over hard cuts in FULL sequences.
- **Results in, results out:** show the output/model, then remove or transform it as the next line starts.
