## 1. The idea
Keep Valeri's current look (light dotted grid, real UIs in rounded frames, red accent) and raise it to a premium, cinematic, continuous edit: slow controlled zooms, seamless transitions, real products and real screen recordings, with a few selective Minecraft/pixel moments.

## 2. What we deliver
- Motion-graphic clips only. The editor assembles the final video.
- TOP (split screen) 1080 × 960, Valeri in the bottom half · FULL 1080 × 1920.
- Headline typography only; the editor adds the word captions (grey rounded box, as in his current videos).
- Per video: preview, transparent overlay (HEVC), edit notes with timecodes, CLONE.mp3 of his on-screen lines.

## 3. Base rules (same as all clients)
- New graphic every ~1.8 s; something moves inside a graphic every 0.5–1 s; nothing static longer than ~4 s.
- Never more than 2 split (TOP) graphics in a row. At least 40 % FULL. Big reveals always FULL.
- Hook: strong, with several visual changes, and no cut until the first sentence ends.
- If he names something, show that exact thing. Numbers always count up. Several things in one line → one visual per word.
- Real assets only: real logos, UIs, product visuals and screen recordings. Never fake a UI or redraw a logo. Never invent numbers.

#### Safe zone
Important graphics and text in the white area; red only if really needed. FULL: y 211–1464 (right edge 1025 above y 938, 915 below). TOP: y 211–938, x 55–1025.

## 6. Typography
- Sans: Inter Display 700–800, tight tracking. Two-tone lines: light grey regular + bold on the key word (“First, the model cuts”).
- Emphasis: Playfair Display Italic in red (“A fresh skill”, “QWEN”).
- Ordinals: grey serif italic (“1st”, “2nd”).
- Pixel lettering only inside Minecraft-style moments.

## 7. Motion
- Zooms: slow and premium. 1.2–2 s ease-in-out, max ~15 %, onto the exact thing (the GitHub repo name, the key line). No fast or generic zooms.
- Cursor: realistic mouse moves to the Claude prompt box, clicks, and the prompt types in.
- Emphasis pop-ups for key phrases like “completely free” or “[amount]”.
- Seamless, continuous edit: zoom-through and match transitions; smooth white → black around a logo zoom. It should feel like one intentional sequence, not separate clips.
- Results in, results out: show the output or the models, then clear or transform them as the narration moves on.
- Signature entry (house): rises from below while blurred, then snaps into focus.
- Never: spins, bouncy text, glitch, cheap fast zooms.

## 9. Minecraft / pixel vibe (selective)
- Use a Minecraft-style visual language only where it fits the story: a process with steps, a “build” moment, a playful contrast. About 1–3 moments per video.
- Don't force it everywhere. It should add visual interest or reinforce the point.
- Keep it premium and intentional: pixel icons on his clean grid/white background next to real UIs. Not overly game-like.

## 11. Don't do
- Generic, fast or cheap zooms.
- Disconnected clips with hard cuts where a transition would flow.
- Fake UIs or mocked-up products.
- Minecraft style everywhere or anything that looks like a game HUD.
- White pill / text box with a green-check icon (“✓ already built”, “✓ it works”). It looks like generic AI-template editing. Use his headline typography instead: bold sans + one red serif-italic word (“already built”, “it works”), no box.
