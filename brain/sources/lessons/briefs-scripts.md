# PM lessons: Briefs & scripts

Mistakes the PM made once and the rule that came out of each.

## A brand describing its review process is not an approval
When: A brand email mentions script review, approval or filming as steps still to come, before any script was sent
Do: Count a script as approved only when the brand approves a script we actually sent (Script Sent Date or a sent Script email). 'Upon our approval', 'send us the script for review' and a step-by-step guide are instructions: answer what they ask for now.
Never: Set Script Approved, move a card to 6 or draft a tool-access email when no script has been sent.
Seen 2 time(s). Source: System check.
## Include the script link in the draft email
When: Sending a script to the brand for review
Do: Add the Google Drive or Frame.io link to the script in the email body so the brand can access it immediately.
Never: Send a script email without the link to the actual file
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Draft edit.
## A script the creator's team sends by hand is a sent script
When: Our side (the creator or their team) emails the script into the brand's thread before the PM's own Script email goes out
Do: Record it like the PM's own send: Script Sent Date and Last Our Reply from that email, Next Action 'waiting on the brand to approve', and retire the PM's waiting Script draft.
Never: Leave the deal saying 'send the script' or with an empty Script Sent Date, because follow-ups and the 'script at 5 with no email' check then work from wrong facts.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## No team ping for work that hasn't started
When: A brand asks to pause, or any update arrives, on a deal with no brief and no video card yet
Do: Keep the hold on the deal and keep following up with the brand, but only tell the script writer or an editor when a video card exists for that deal, meaning work was actually handed to them.
Never: Never ping Rudraksh or an editor to stop or change work on a video they were never given.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Scripts at Script Approval go to the brand
When: A card reaches 5- Script Approval
Do: Share the script with the brand in the deal's existing thread (WhatsApp deals: tell Harsh). When a sponsor card reaches 5 and no deal links to it, find the brand's open deal and link the card to it instead of only alerting "script ready, no deal".
Never: Leave it unsent or send it in a new thread.
Seen 2 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## An approval with the brand's own script is an approval
When: Brand/platform says 'Approved Idea, Script', 'validated version', or Next Step 'Draft video'
Do: Card to To Film, deal Script Approved, save their version as the final script.
Never: Send it to Rudraksh as changes or a brief update.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## No money for the team
When: Writing cards, briefs, team pings, the access page
Do: Keep only creative content.
Never: Mention a rate, price, fee, budget, $ or payment.
Seen 3 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## One video card per reel
When: A deal covers 2+ reels, or a brief arrives for the next reel
Do: Make a card per reel; put the next reel's brief on its own card.
Never: Put reel 2's brief on reel 1's finished card.
Seen 3 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
