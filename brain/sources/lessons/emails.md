# PM lessons: Emails

Mistakes the PM made once and the rule that came out of each.

## A brand asking us to sign an agreement always gets the fixed contract reply, even when it mentions a countersigned copy or the deal is already finished
When: A brand emails an agreement for us to sign
Do: File it in CONTRACTS, send it to Harsh to sign, and send contract_ack in the thread. A new agreement on a deal past delivery is the next campaign
Never: Treat 'we will provide the countersigned copy' as a signed copy, or block the reply because the deal is at Invoiced or Paid
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## No duplicate posted links emails when already sent
When: A posted links email is being drafted
Do: Check if the live link or announcement was already sent in the thread before drafting a posted links email; drop the draft if it was already sent.
Never: Send a posted links email when the link was already sent in that thread
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Draft rejected.
## A revised cut that is ready goes to the brand; the brand's other questions never hold it
When: The editor has finished the brand's change notes and the cut email is checked, while the brand's latest email also asks something else (a live link, an ad code, a date)
Do: Send the cut email naming the changes it carries, and send the other ask to Harsh as its own owed ask. A cut email only hands over the cut (rule 62).
Never: Never flag or hold a cut email with 'Does not answer: <another ask>', and never claim the cut lacks changes the editor marked done (Changes Done At after the notes).
Seen 2 time(s). Source: System check.
## "Shared it with the client" on a cut gets the fixed review reply
When: The brand says our video draft is with their client or team for review, and asks nothing else
Do: Send the draft_review_ack fixed reply (we'll wait for the feedback, the editor turns changes around in 1 to 2 working days).
Never: Never log it as 'no reply needed' and leave the brand on read.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Two brand emails minutes apart get one answer, and it follows the newest one
When: A brand sends a second email (a correction, an extra note, a change of plan) minutes after the first, before or while our fixed acknowledgement goes out
Do: Read every brand email in the burst and answer the newest one once. If the newest says they no longer need to see a revised script or cut, say so back and put the next real step (the design preview, filming) in the reply; any money or partnership ask in it (a community post, a Skool collab) goes to Harsh.
Never: Never send the same acknowledgement twice in a thread within minutes, and never promise a revised script or cut the brand's latest email said they do not need to see.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A brand's 'thanks for confirming, send the link once it's live' needs no reply
When: The brand's newest email only confirms what we already said (a posting date, a plan) and asks for something we will deliver later, like the live link
Do: Write nothing now; the email that carries the live link after posting is the reply.
Never: Never draft a reply that just repeats our own promise back ("we'll send the link once it's posted on the 30th").
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A brand's status update gets one acknowledgement per round, never one per email
When: The brand says the cut or script is with their team, sends a thumbs-up, asks us to wait, or the same email lands again in the creator's inbox, and we already acknowledged since our last real email
Do: Send the stage's fixed acknowledgement once; after that, a status update, emoji or 'please wait' needs no reply until the brand sends feedback or a question
Never: Send the same 'we'll wait for the feedback' email twice in a thread, or draft a 'we are standing by' reply
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A reply never says something is already set up or done unless someone on our side said so
When: A brand asks us to confirm a setup step (comment-DM automation, access, upload, the release file) before posting
Do: Answer with what will be done, or hold the draft for Harsh to confirm the fact; facts come only from the thread, the card or Harsh's words
Never: Write 'we have the DM automation set up', 'access is working' or 'it's uploaded' when nobody on our side said so
Seen 2 time(s). Source: System check.
## An agreement the brand accepts by an email reply is never answered with 'we'll sign it'
When: A brand attaches an agreement or rules and says a written reply such as "Agree with the Influencer Cooperation Rules" is enough and no signature is needed
Do: File the agreement, send Harsh the file saying no signature is needed and that agreeing is his reply, and leave the written 'Agree' reply plus any account email to him
Never: Send the contract_ack ('we've received it and will review and sign it') or tell Harsh to sign it when the brand says no signature is needed
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A brand asking why we did something gets Harsh's answer, never a made-up reason
When: A brand questions one of our own actions ('why was a script shared before the agreement?', 'why did you post early?') or asks about the creator's intent
Do: Answer the parts the thread already settles (e.g. noting their payment terms) only if Harsh adds the rest; put the 'why' and the creator's intent to Harsh on WhatsApp and draft from his words.
Never: Invent a motive ('to keep the momentum going'), speak for the creator's intent, or promise to wait on a brief nobody discussed.
Seen 5 time(s). Source: System check.
## Every new brand email with notes gets its fixed acknowledgement, every round
When: The brand sends round 2 (or later) notes on a script or video within 3 days of the round 1 acknowledgement
Do: Send the fixed acknowledgement again; only a copy written after this brand email counts as already answered
Never: Skip an acknowledgement because the same template went in the thread in the last 3 days for an earlier round
Seen 3 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## The script-approval email sends on its own, even when the brand lists points
When: A brand approves a script and also asks us to confirm something (people, facts, permissions)
Do: Thank them, say we'll film and take care of all the points they listed, ask for access on noocaptools@gmail.com, and send it on its own with no second-check hold
Never: Hold the script-approval / tool-access email for Harsh or ask him on WhatsApp about the brand's filming points. Harsh, Nov 26: 'these kind of mails don't need my approval, it should send straight away'
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Never tell a brand the video is published or live without its live link
When: Replying to a brand that cleared the video for posting or asked to publish it
Do: Say only what is true now. If the video is live, give the live post link; if it is not, leave posting out (the post date is Harsh's call).
Never: Write 'we have published the video', 'it is now live' or 'the reel has been posted' when there is no live post link and the deal is not at Posted.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## Never quote a brand a price nobody gave
When: A brand asks about a rate, a package or how things are progressing on a deal still in negotiation, and the price is not in the thread, the deal or Harsh's WhatsApp answer
Do: Use only a price that is written in the thread, on the deal or in Harsh's answer, word for word. If our side already sent a price, the deal is waiting on the brand. If no price exists, ask Harsh once on WhatsApp and draft nothing until he answers.
Never: Invent, round up, combine or estimate a price, a package total or a fee in any email.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.

When: A brand email says it is waiting for us to confirm something before it moves (once you confirm the go-live date, please confirm Chris is available on the 3rd)
Do: Mark it as needing our info and ask Harsh once on WhatsApp for the confirmation, quoting the brand's sentence
Never: Never treat it as a plain brief or brand reply that needs nothing from us, and never answer the date ourselves

## Keep chasing until the brand answers or pays
When: A brand goes quiet on a follow-up, an on-hold check-in or an unpaid invoice

## Never send an email that says nothing beyond a greeting, thanks and a sign-off
When: A guard takes made-up details, filler or a foreign link out of a draft and little is left, or a reply is written to a brand email that only acknowledges something or asks for a fact only Harsh has
Do: Write the email again saying what it is for and leave out only the part we have no facts for; the missing fact goes to Harsh. If the brand only acknowledged ("sounds good", "thanks for the update"), write no reply at all. If the brand asked for something only Harsh has (an account email, a date), ask Harsh instead of sending thanks.
Never: Never save or send a draft whose body is just 'Hi X,' and 'Best, X's Team', or only 'thanks / great news / we'll keep an eye out / we look forward / we saw your message / it was a pleasure working with you'
Seen 4 time(s). Source: System check.
## A draft the last send check refuses goes to Harsh once, it is not retried forever
When: Autopilot tries to send a waiting draft and a pre-send check refuses it ("Not sent: ...", e.g. the scripts already went, someone already replied, wrong amount)
Do: Mark the draft Needs you with the reason the check gave and post one hold card, so Harsh decides and Approvals stops saying it will send on its own
Never: Leave the draft saying "Sends on its own in 30 min" while every heartbeat retries it and fails the same way
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.

## Each follow-up is worded fresh, never a copy of the one before
When: Writing a second or later follow-up on the same deal
Do: Read our previous follow-ups in the thread and word the new one differently: name what we sent and when, and ask one clear question (approved, or changes?)
Never: Never send a follow-up whose text matches our previous email word for word, and never add an offer to cancel the project, which is Harsh's call
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
