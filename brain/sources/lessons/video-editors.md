# PM lessons: Video & editors

Mistakes the PM made once and the rule that came out of each.

## Every brand approval reaches the editor's thread, even when the video already has a thread
When: A brand approves a cut and the PM moves the EDIT TRACKER row to To Post
Do: Send the APPROVED alert into the editor's video thread for each new approval, with the brand's note (publish date, code, version) in it
Never: Treat the approval time itself as proof the editor was already told
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## No payment or contract line ever goes into a video card's Brand Notes, however it is phrased
When: Saving what a brand said onto the deal's video cards (Brand Notes field and the dated note inside the card)
Do: Keep only notes about the video itself (hashtags, ad code, posting window, creative asks). Payment timing, invoices, bank details and contract terms stay on the deal and go to Harsh.
Never: Never write lines like 'Payment will be made within 7 days after publishing' or 'Brand received the ACH routing number' on a card the writer, editors or creator can see.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A cut the PM could not send waits for Harsh, it is never re-sent on its own every run
When: A revised cut at Send to Brand is held by a check or blocked (no deal, no link, no brand email) and the row goes back to In Review
Do: Leave the row at In Review with its 'Not sent' note and one alert to Harsh; resend only when Harsh switches it to Send to Brand again
Never: Never treat the PM's own 'Not sent' edit as the editor finishing a new round, and never post the same 'to approve' or 'couldn't send' alert again each heartbeat
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## Brand changes reach the editor once per round, in the brand's own words
When: A brand asks for video changes (any round)
Do: Let the CHANGES REQUESTED alert carry the notes; the PM reposts only when Feedback Posted is still unticked after 20 minutes. Store the brand's feedback, or the summary only when there is no feedback text
Never: Post the PM's 'Brand changes · round N' message as well as the alert, or save the feedback plus its summary (every note shows twice)
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Every editor ping says in words whether the video is a sponsor, trial or personal
When: Any new-video ping to an editor's Discord channel
Do: Put a Type line under the title (SPONSOR video, the brand reviews this cut / TRIAL REEL / Personal) and read the exact TYPE select, which wins over other columns named Type
Never: Rely on the badge emoji alone, or read the first column called Type (Chris has a file column and Brad a content-category column with the same name)
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## A brand video is SPONSOR in the edit tracker, whatever the board's TYPE looks like
When: An editor is assigned to a video and its EDIT TRACKER row is created
Do: Read TYPE as a select or a multi-select; if it's empty, a card with a Brand Email or a 'Brand × Creator' title is SPONSOR.
Never: Never default a brand video to PERSONAL just because TYPE couldn't be read.
Seen 3 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## One brand email about several videos is split by video
When: A brand reviews more than one video of a deal in one email ("Video 1: ... Video 2: ...") and each video has its own editor
Do: Give each video only its own section: changes go to that video's editor, an approved video goes to To Post with its hashtags and links as the Post Caption.
Never: Never send the whole email to the first video found, and never treat a mixed email as all changes or all approved.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## An editor's 'done' counts even after they pressed 'started edit'
When: An editor types done in a video thread while the EDIT TRACKER row shows Manual editing / Claude running / Editor review because they pressed 'started' on a changes round
Do: Treat done as the changes being finished whenever changes are still open (Changes Requested At after Changes Done At): brand rounds go to Send to Brand, internal rounds to In Review.
Never: Never ignore a done just because the row left Changes when the editor started working.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Several cuts of one deal go in one email, each link named by its video
When: Two or more videos of the same deal are switched to Send to Brand together, or a cut of a multi-video deal is sent
Do: Send one email with every link labelled by its video number (Video 1: link, Video 2: link). A single cut of a multi-video deal names its own number.
Never: Never send two cut emails seconds apart, and never call a video 'the first' unless it is video 1.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## A platform's 'submit it in the app' notice is not change notes
When: Faved, creator.co or another platform emails that the draft must be submitted through their campaign page, or that a submission had no asset
Do: Tell Harsh once to submit the cut on the platform; keep the tracker row where it is
Never: Never put the row on Changes or ping the editor with CHANGES REQUESTED for platform instructions
Seen 2 time(s). Source: System check.
## One Drive folders post per card
When: The heartbeat makes Drive folders for a card at To Film
Do: Check the live card for links before making folders and posting to Discord
Never: Trust the board snapshot alone and post 'Drive folders ready' again for a card that already has its links
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## Hashtags are the post caption, not changes to the cut
When: A brand whose reel is approved or cleared to post asks only for different hashtags or caption tags
Do: Keep the video where it is (To Post), put the hashtags on the card's Post Caption, tell whoever posts, and get the reel live with them, then send the live link.
Never: Move the tracker row or card back to Changes or send hashtag notes to the editor as video feedback.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A deleted video card never stops a brand email from being handled
When: A brand email arrives for a deal whose linked card was deleted
Do: Skip the note on the deleted card and handle the rest of the email
Never: Let a Notion 'block is archived' error fail the whole email
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A brand's publish deadline means the video is ready for their review 4 days before it
When: A brand gives a publish, go-live or posting deadline, asks about the timeline, or worries the deadline is tight
Do: Work back from the deadline: the video must be ready for the brand's review 3 to 4 days before the publish date (4 by default), so there is time for their review, a round of changes and posting. When several videos can't go live on the same day, plan them on separate days that all land by the deadline. Replies about timing say when the scripts and the videos will be with them, worked out this way
Never: Never plan the video to be ready on the publish deadline itself, and never answer a deadline worry with only 'that can be done' and no dates
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Drive links appear within 10 minutes of To Film
When: A card moves to 6- To Film
Do: Make the Raw Footage, Assets and Edited Vid links on the next heartbeat.
Never: Never leave a To Film card without links for more than one heartbeat.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Images sent with change notes go to the editor too
When: Harsh forwards change notes on WhatsApp with an image (a logo, a screenshot of the shot to fix)
Do: Send the image to the editor with the notes: into their Discord thread, under the notes on the intake row and on the video card.
Never: Never pass on only the words when the message carried an image the editor needs.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## One brand email can carry two asks, and both get acted on
When: A brand email does more than one thing, like approving video 1 while sending notes on scripts 2 and 3
Do: Act on every ask in it: the approved video's tracker row goes to To Post and the script notes go to the script cards. Anything not acted on goes to Harsh in #pm and the email stays unread
Never: File the whole email under one event and drop the other ask
Seen 2 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## One ping per card, and check what fires before changing anything by hand
When: Claude or the team makes a card, moves a row or queues a message by hand
Do: First work out which PM or n8n step reacts to that change (new sponsor card = Rudraksh is pinged on the next run) and let that one step do the announcing
Never: Queue a second ping for something an automation will already announce
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Claude.
## A script-writer flag belongs to the card it was picked on
When: Send to Script Writer (Script changes, Brief changed, Concept changed) is picked on one card of a deal with several videos
Do: Move, note and link only that card. Leave the other videos' cards, and the deal's Next Action, as they are unless the flagged card is the deal's own linked video.
Never: Never move a card whose cut is with the brand back to 4- Script Draft, and never write another video's brief onto it.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A cut is 'revised' only if the brand already saw a cut
When: Writing the email that hands a cut to the brand after our own team or Harsh asked for changes before the brand saw anything
Do: Call it the cut for their review. Say it is the revised cut with their changes only when the brand saw an earlier cut and asked for those changes
Never: Never tell a brand 'here is the revised cut with the changes you asked for' when they never saw a cut or asked for changes
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A brand approving the cut moves the tracker row to To Post, even when the second reader only argues about the label
When: A brand writes that the draft or cut is approved ("looks great, approved!", "go ahead and publish"), even while asking for the remaining assets or a final check
Do: Read it as video_feedback with video_approved: move the EDIT TRACKER row to To Post with Approved At, and stop chasing the brand for feedback on that cut
Never: Never hold a video approval just because the second reader calls it a plain brand_reply, and never leave the row on With Brand after the brand approved it
Seen 3 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Notes on an edited cut go to the editor, never the script writer
When: Harsh forwards or types fixes with timestamps, shots, avatar, music or captions, often with a second line naming the deal ("Brad x promo cut changes")
Do: Read messages sent together as one; send cut notes to the named deal's EDIT TRACKER row as Changes so the editor is pinged
Never: Send cut notes to Rudraksh as script changes, guess the deal from the last card in the chat, or treat a deal label as the answer a brand is waiting for
Seen 2 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## A cut link after script approval is a Draft video email
When: Someone on WhatsApp or chat hands over an edited video link (Drive, Frame.io) to send to a brand whose script is already approved
Do: File it as Kind 'Draft video' so the deal moves to Submitted and the board card moves to 9- Approval Brand/Creator, and write one sentence that hands over the cut
Never: File a cut as a Script email or ask for 'feedback on the script' once the script is approved
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A revised cut email only hands over the cut
When: Sending a cut to a brand, above all a revised cut after their changes
Do: Say in one sentence that the link is the revised cut with the changes they asked for, and nothing else. A billing or other question the brand asked in the same email goes to Harsh (WhatsApp) or its own reply.
Never: Answer the brand's earlier email inside the cut email, promise the revised cut 'for a final check' when this IS the revised cut, or ask a brand that pays through a platform (Gusto) or already got 3 billing reminders for their billing details. When the brand asks US to send billing details, never reply by asking for theirs.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## Approved with one change means changes for the editor
When: A brand approves a cut but asks for one change in the same email (music, a caption, a frame)
Do: Put the video's EDIT TRACKER row on Changes with the brand's words in Brand Feedback and the board card on 8- Changes, so the editor fixes it and the revised cut goes straight back to the brand. Anything else in the email (billing details, a date) is handled on its own.
Never: Hold the whole email for Harsh because the readers disagree on approval vs feedback, or treat it as a full approval and post the cut unchanged.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## One EDIT TRACKER row per video
When: Adding or creating an EDIT TRACKER row for a video (by hand, from a brief page, or when an edit was delivered without the buttons)
Do: Look up existing rows with the same Video ID first. If one exists, update that row (status, dates, note) instead of adding a new one.
Never: Add a second row for a Video ID that already has one; the watcher, follow-ups and reports then count the same cut twice.
Seen 2 time(s). Source: System check.
## Let the n8n watcher send the editor's changes alert
When: The PM sets an EDIT TRACKER row to Changes from a brand email
Do: Set Status to Changes and fill Brand Feedback only. Leave Changes Requested At empty: the n8n EDIT TRACKER watcher sends the tagged 'CHANGES REQUESTED' alert within 2 minutes and stamps that field itself. The PM's own thread post is only a fallback after 20 minutes.
Never: Never stamp Changes Requested At from the PM (it makes the watcher think the alert already went out), and never ask Harsh to add settings to fix something that used to work: find what changed.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Brand changes move the board card to 8
When: A brand sends change notes on a cut that is with them
Do: Set the EDIT TRACKER row to Changes, ping the editor, and move the creator's board card back to 8- Changes at the same moment.
Never: Never leave the board card on 9- Approval while the editor is working on brand changes.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## An approved video goes live inside the brand's window
When: A brand approves a cut and names publishing days
Do: The same day, put the card at 10- To Post with a POST DATE inside the brand's window and tell the team. If the date passes with nothing live, tell Harsh once with the brand's latest message. Take the invoice due date from the contract terms (Net 30 = invoice date + 30), not the Stripe default.
Never: Let an approved video sit unposted for days while the brand chases, or record it only as 'Waiting on us' with no card or date.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## Cut with the brand means board card at 9
When: An edited cut goes to the brand, by Send to Brand or by approving a Draft video email
Do: Move the creator's board card to 9- Approval Brand/Creator together with the EDIT TRACKER row going to With Brand, and only move it forward from 6, 7 or 8.
Never: Never leave the board card on 7- In Edit while the video is waiting on the brand.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Editor page shows the approved script
When: An editor opens a video's brief page
Do: Show the approved version from the Script sub-page (the version marked approved/final, else the latest).
Never: Show no script or an old version.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## Editors get the brand's full notes
When: Passing brand notes to an editor
Do: Send every line, and the Discord Changes alert must show them under 'What to fix'.
Never: Send only the first line, or an alert without the notes.
Seen 2 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## Every Raw Footage folder is shared by link
When: A Drive folder is made for a video
Do: Share it with anyone with the link before giving it out.
Never: Hand out a private folder.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## A cut can't wait unseen in review
When: A cut is In Review on EDIT TRACKER
Do: Remind once a day, and right away when the go-live date is close.
Never: Let it sit until the post date passes.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## Each video's cut email stands alone
When: Sending or drafting a cut for one video
Do: Only replace an older cut email for the same video (same Frame.io link or title).
Never: Reject another video's revised cut.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## Editor page reads each board's own column names
When: Showing card fields on the editor brief page
Do: Match REF, Ref Video, Edited Vid and similar.
Never: Assume one column name for every board.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## Brand change notes go to the editor
When: A brand sends notes on a cut or a script
Do: Put the notes on the right video row and send them to the editor (cut) or the script writer (script). Reply to the brand with the fixed acknowledgement from Reply Templates (video_changes_ack / script_changes_ack: thanks, revised cut or script in 1 to 2 working days), every round, on its own. After the editor is done, send the revised-cut email.
Never: Write an AI reply to change notes (no promises of reshoots, new presenters or 'we'll check with our editor'). The fixed acknowledgement is NOT this: it always goes (Harsh, Nov 26).
Seen 3 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
