# PM lessons: Video & editors

Mistakes the PM made once and the rule that came out of each.

## A brand approving the cut moves the tracker row to To Post, even when the second reader only argues about the label
When: A brand writes that the draft or cut is approved ("looks great, approved!", "go ahead and publish"), even while asking for the remaining assets or a final check
Do: Read it as video_feedback with video_approved: move the EDIT TRACKER row to To Post with Approved At, and stop chasing the brand for feedback on that cut
Never: Never hold a video approval just because the second reader calls it a plain brand_reply, and never leave the row on With Brand after the brand approved it
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Notes on an edited cut go to the editor, never the script writer
When: Harsh forwards or types fixes with timestamps, shots, avatar, music or captions, often with a second line naming the deal ("Brad x promo cut changes")
Do: Read messages sent together as one; send cut notes to the named deal's EDIT TRACKER row as Changes so the editor is pinged
Never: Send cut notes to Rudraksh as script changes, guess the deal from the last card in the chat, or treat a deal label as the answer a brand is waiting for
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## A cut link after script approval is a Draft video email
When: Someone on WhatsApp or chat hands over an edited video link (Drive, Frame.io) to send to a brand whose script is already approved
Do: File it as Kind 'Draft video' so the deal moves to Submitted and the board card moves to 9- Approval Brand/Creator, and write one sentence that hands over the cut
Never: File a cut as a Script email or ask for 'feedback on the script' once the script is approved
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A revised cut email only hands over the cut
When: Sending a cut to a brand, above all a revised cut after their changes
Do: Say in one sentence that the link is the revised cut with the changes they asked for, and nothing else. A billing or other question the brand asked in the same email goes to Harsh (WhatsApp) or its own reply.
Never: Answer the brand's earlier email inside the cut email, promise the revised cut 'for a final check' when this IS the revised cut, or ask a brand that pays through a platform (Gusto) or already got 3 billing reminders for their billing details. When the brand asks US to send billing details, never reply by asking for theirs.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## Approved with one change means changes for the editor
When: A brand approves a cut but asks for one change in the same email (music, a caption, a frame)
Do: Put the video's EDIT TRACKER row on Changes with the brand's words in Brand Feedback and the board card on 8- Changes, so the editor fixes it and the revised cut goes straight back to the brand. Anything else in the email (billing details, a date) is handled on its own.
Never: Hold the whole email for Harsh because the readers disagree on approval vs feedback, or treat it as a full approval and post the cut unchanged.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## One EDIT TRACKER row per video
When: Adding or creating an EDIT TRACKER row for a video (by hand, from a brief page, or when an edit was delivered without the buttons)
Do: Look up existing rows with the same Video ID first. If one exists, update that row (status, dates, note) instead of adding a new one.
Never: Add a second row for a Video ID that already has one; the watcher, follow-ups and reports then count the same cut twice.
Seen 2 time(s). Source: System check.
## Let the n8n watcher send the editor's changes alert
When: The PM sets an EDIT TRACKER row to Changes from a brand email
Do: Set Status to Changes and fill Brand Feedback only. Leave Changes Requested At empty: the n8n EDIT TRACKER watcher sends the tagged 'CHANGES REQUESTED' alert within 2 minutes and stamps that field itself. The PM's own thread post is only a fallback after 20 minutes.
Never: Never stamp Changes Requested At from the PM (it makes the watcher think the alert already went out), and never ask Harsh to add settings to fix something that used to work: find what changed.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Brand changes move the board card to 8
When: A brand sends change notes on a cut that is with them
Do: Set the EDIT TRACKER row to Changes, ping the editor, and move the creator's board card back to 8- Changes at the same moment.
Never: Never leave the board card on 9- Approval while the editor is working on brand changes.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## An approved video goes live inside the brand's window
When: A brand approves a cut and names publishing days
Do: The same day, put the card at 10- To Post with a POST DATE inside the brand's window and tell the team. If the date passes with nothing live, tell Harsh once with the brand's latest message. Take the invoice due date from the contract terms (Net 30 = invoice date + 30), not the Stripe default.
Never: Let an approved video sit unposted for days while the brand chases, or record it only as 'Waiting on us' with no card or date.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## Cut with the brand means board card at 9
When: An edited cut goes to the brand, by Send to Brand or by approving a Draft video email
Do: Move the creator's board card to 9- Approval Brand/Creator together with the EDIT TRACKER row going to With Brand, and only move it forward from 6, 7 or 8.
Never: Never leave the board card on 7- In Edit while the video is waiting on the brand.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Harsh correction.
## Editor page shows the approved script
When: An editor opens a video's brief page
Do: Show the approved version from the Script sub-page (the version marked approved/final, else the latest).
Never: Show no script or an old version.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## Editors get the brand's full notes
When: Passing brand notes to an editor
Do: Send every line, and the Discord Changes alert must show them under 'What to fix'.
Never: Send only the first line, or an alert without the notes.
Seen 2 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## Every Raw Footage folder is shared by link
When: A Drive folder is made for a video
Do: Share it with anyone with the link before giving it out.
Never: Hand out a private folder.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## A cut can't wait unseen in review
When: A cut is In Review on EDIT TRACKER
Do: Remind once a day, and right away when the go-live date is close.
Never: Let it sit until the post date passes.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## Each video's cut email stands alone
When: Sending or drafting a cut for one video
Do: Only replace an older cut email for the same video (same Frame.io link or title).
Never: Reject another video's revised cut.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## Editor page reads each board's own column names
When: Showing card fields on the editor brief page
Do: Match REF, Ref Video, Edited Vid and similar.
Never: Assume one column name for every board.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
## Brand change notes go to the editor
When: A brand sends notes on a cut or a script
Do: Put the notes on the right video row and send them to the editor (cut) or Rudraksh (script). After the editor is done, draft the revised-cut email.
Never: Reply to the brand just to acknowledge the notes.
Seen 2 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
