# PM lessons: Deals & data

Mistakes the PM made once and the rule that came out of each.

## Inbox reader lists can arrive as one string
When: Filing an email from the reader's answer
Do: Treat summary, we_promised and delivers as lists whatever shape the reader returns
Never: Let one oddly shaped field stop an email from being filed
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A brand saying 'got it, we'll review' leaves the next move with the brand
When: A brand writes again only to confirm they received or can open what we sent and will come back with feedback
Do: Record it as the brand's latest email and set the deal to waiting on the brand, with the next check-in counted from that email
Never: Set the deal to 'Waiting on us', put it on Harsh's list or draft a reply to a plain acknowledgement
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## Only our own inboxes speak for NOOCAP
When: Reading an email in a deal thread that is signed by someone at an outside company (an agency or the brand's team)
Do: Treat anyone writing from a company domain that is not noocapcollab or a creator's own domain as the brand's side: record it as a brand reply, move Last Brand Reply, and let their address become the brand contact.
Never: File an agency's 'script is under review' or 'waiting for the client's approval' as our own email because the signer's first name matches one of our creators or the tone sounds like ours.
Seen 2 time(s). Source: System check.
## A brand email in the creator's inbox belongs to the deal of that brand contact
When: A brand on a live deal writes only to the creator's own inbox (no reply-all), so the email has another Gmail thread id than the deal
Do: Match the deal by the sender's address (the deal's Brand Email) and creator, record the email on that deal, set Last Brand Reply and hand it to Harsh as Waiting on us; link a locked lead to the deal of that contact before any brand-name match. When that email approves the script, act on it as an approval: deal to Script Approved with the date, card to 6- To Film, and the tool-access email in the deal's own thread.
Never: Leave the deal on 'waiting for the brand' because only the lead was updated, or link a lead to another deal because the brand names overlap (Tripo 3D vs Hyper3D / Tripo 3D)
Seen 2 time(s). Source: System check.
## A new deal in an old thread starts with nothing from the old deal's billing
When: Opening a deal for a brand's next campaign or a new brief that arrived in an existing thread
Do: Start it at Inbound (or the stage the new emails really show) with empty invoice fields, its own Deal Memory about the new campaign only, and the brand's newest Message-ID. Billing, invoices and payment chases stay on the old deal.
Never: Copy the old deal's stage, Invoice Sent Date, Invoice Amount, invoice number or 'waiting for payment' memory onto the new deal; a copied Invoice Sent Date starts payment chases for money nobody owes.
Seen 2 time(s). Source: System check.
## An invite to the brand's next campaign is a new deal
When: A brand whose deal is posted, invoiced or paid invites the creator to a new campaign in the same thread, even inside an email about payment and without asking for a rate
Do: Open a deal of its own at Inbound (same thread, Needs Check), ask Harsh on WhatsApp whether the creator joins, and keep the payment news on the old deal
Never: Fold the invite into the finished deal's memory or Next Action, or treat it as a brief on the old deal
Seen 2 time(s). Source: System check.
## A rate ask on a finished deal is a new inquiry
When: A brand whose deal is already Posted, Invoiced or Paid writes in the same thread asking for our rate for something new (another video, an event, a session).
Do: Open a separate deal at Inbound for the new ask with the thread's IDs, and put the rate question to Harsh. Leave the finished deal's memory and stage as they were.
Never: Never fold the new ask into the finished deal or rewrite its Deal Memory, and never guess a rate.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A deal opened from our own handover email still needs the brand's address
When: The creator's team writes 'please add noocapcollab to this thread' and the PM opens a deal from that email
Do: Set Brand Email to the brand person our email was written to (first To/Cc address that is not ours, the creator's or a no-reply robot), and copy the Thread CC
Never: Leave Brand Email empty because the email came from our side, so no reply or script can be addressed later
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## A brand question is not a hold
When: A brand asks us a question or says they could not do something on their side
Do: Treat it as a question we owe an answer to (ask Harsh once if only he knows) and keep the deal moving.
Never: Label it 'brand asked us to hold the work' or send a hold alert when the brand never said to pause.
Seen 1 time(s). Source: System check.
## Read the whole thread before acting
When: Any brand email, especially agency threads
Do: Read the full conversation: a new brand in an agency thread is a new deal, earlier proof of payment counts, amounts come from what was agreed.
Never: Judge an email on its own.
Seen 4 time(s). Source: Rulebook.
