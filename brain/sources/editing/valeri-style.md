## The feel (from his videos + inspiration)
- **Premium, cinematic, controlled.** Polished and high-end, never generic or busy (i05).
- **One continuous edit, not separate clips.** Graphics hand over to each other: a logo zooms into the next scene, outputs appear then get cleared as the narration moves on, white flows into black (i02).
- **Multiple visual changes in the hook** to hold retention (i01).

## Fonts
- Sans: **Inter Display** 700–800, tight tracking (i02 "First, the model **cuts**": light grey regular + bold white/black on the key word).
- Emphasis: **Playfair Display Italic** in `accent` red ("*A fresh skill*", “QWEN”, as in o02/o03).
- Ordinals: serif italic in grey ("*1st*", "*2nd*"), as in o01.
- Pixel accent: **Press Start 2P** / pixel lettering only inside Minecraft-style moments (see below).

## Motion (Valeri-specific)
- **Zoom: slow and premium.** Ease-in-out over 1.2–2 s, scale 1 → 1.15 max, onto the exact thing (the GitHub repo name, the key line). **No fast or generic punch-zooms.**
- **Cursor clicks:** realistic macOS cursor moves to the field, clicks (press + ripple), and the prompt types in (i01).
- **Emphasis pop-ups** for key phrases ("completely free", "$0"): a card or stamp springs in over the scene, holds, then clears.
- **Seamless transitions:** match-cut and zoom-through between scenes; white ↔ black via a logo zoom (i02). Prefer these over hard cuts in FULL sequences.
- **Results in, results out:** show the output/model, then remove or transform it as the next line starts.

## Minecraft / pixel accents (m01, m02, i06)
- Pixel-art icons in a chunky framed tile (m01), a pixel mascot (m02), pixel lettering "CLAUDE CODE" (i06).
- **Use selectively:** 1–3 moments per video where it reinforces the point (a process with steps, a "build" moment, a playful contrast). Never the whole video.
- Keep it premium: pixel icons sit on his clean grid/white background with real UIs nearby; no game HUDs, no 8-bit sound or jitter.

## Don't
- **No white pill / text box with a green-check icon** ("✓ already built", "✓ it works"). It reads as generic AI-template editing. Use his headline typography instead: bold sans + one red serif-italic word ("already *built*", "it *works*"), no box.
