NOOCAP Manager · Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

NOOCAP Manager is an internal operations assistant run by NOOCAP Media, a partnership firm based in Mumbai, India. It is used only by the NOOCAP Media team to receive work updates and manage internal tasks over WhatsApp. It is not offered to the public or to customers.

What we collect

We process the WhatsApp phone numbers of our own team members who have been added to the system, along with the messages they send to the NOOCAP Manager number, so that the assistant can reply and carry out the work they ask for.

How we use it

Messages are used only to answer team members' questions and to send them updates about NOOCAP Media's own projects. We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone for marketing, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Message history is kept in NOOCAP Media's private workspace so the assistant can follow the conversation, and is accessible only to the NOOCAP Media team. Messages pass through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform under Meta's own terms.

Deleting your data

Any team member can ask for their message history to be deleted by messaging the NOOCAP Manager number (+91 90820 18486) or the NOOCAP Media team directly, and we will remove it within 30 days.
